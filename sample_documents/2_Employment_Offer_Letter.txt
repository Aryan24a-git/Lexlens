EMPLOYMENT OFFER LETTER

1. POSITION AND DUTIES
Acme Technologies Inc. ("Company") is pleased to offer you the full-time position of Senior Software Engineer, reporting to the VP of Engineering.

2. START DATE AND COMPENSATION
Your employment will commence on November 1, 2026. Your starting annual base salary will be $165,000.00 USD, payable semi-monthly in accordance with standard payroll practices.

3. AT-WILL EMPLOYMENT
Your employment with the Company is for no specified period and constitutes at-will employment. As a result, you are free to resign at any time, for any reason or no reason. Similarly, the Company is free to conclude its employment relationship with you at any time.

4. EQUITY INCENTIVE
Subject to approval by the Board of Directors, you will be granted an option to purchase 15,000 shares of Common Stock under the Company's 2024 Stock Incentive Plan, vesting over four (4) years with a one-year cliff.

5. BENEFITS AND PAID TIME OFF
You will be eligible to participate in the Company's standard employee benefit plans, including medical, dental, vision, and 401(k) matching. You will accrue 15 days of Paid Time Off (PTO) per calendar year.

6. PROPRIETARY INFORMATION AND INVENTIONS
As a condition of employment, you agree to execute the Company's standard Employee Confidential Information and Inventions Assignment Agreement (CIIA), assigning all intellectual property created during employment to the Company.

7. NON-SOLICITATION
During employment and for twelve (12) months following termination of employment, you agree not to directly solicit or induce any employee or contractor of the Company to leave their employment.

8. GOVERNING LAW
This offer letter is governed by the laws of the State of California.
