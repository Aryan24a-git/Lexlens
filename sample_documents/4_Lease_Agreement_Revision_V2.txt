RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT (REVISED DRAFT V2)

1. PREMISES & OCCUPANCY
Landlord hereby leases to Tenant, and Tenant hereby leases from Landlord, the residential dwelling located at 742 Evergreen Terrace, Apartment 4B, Springfield ("Premises"), for use solely as a private residential dwelling.

2. TERM
The term of this Lease shall commence on October 1, 2026 and continue through September 30, 2027 ("Initial Term").

3. RENT & PAYMENTS
Tenant agrees to pay Landlord monthly rent in the amount of $2,500.00 USD, due on the first (1st) day of each calendar month. A grace period of three (3) business days is permitted. If rent is not received by the third (3rd) business day, Tenant shall pay a late fee of $150.00 USD.

4. SECURITY DEPOSIT & DEDUCTIONS
Upon execution of this Lease, Tenant shall deposit with Landlord the sum of $2,500.00 USD as a security deposit. Within thirty (30) days after Tenant vacates the Premises, Landlord shall return the deposit minus damages.

5. USE & CONDUCT
The Premises shall be occupied exclusively by Tenant. Tenant shall not disturb neighbors or conduct unlawful activities.

6. MAINTENANCE & REPAIRS
Tenant is responsible for all minor repairs under $200.00 USD. Landlord maintains major structural components.

7. LANDLORD ENTRY & ACCESS
Landlord may enter the Premises with twenty-four (24) hours notice for inspections or showings.

8. ASSIGNMENT & SUBLETTING
Tenant may not assign or sublet any portion of the Premises under any circumstances.

9. UTILITIES & SERVICES
Tenant shall contract and pay directly for all utilities, including water, sewer, trash, gas, and electricity.

10. DEFAULT & RIGHT TO CURE
If Tenant fails to pay rent, Landlord may deliver a written notice to cure within three (3) days.

11. RENEWAL & TERMINATION NOTICE
Upon expiration, this Lease automatically renews for an additional 12-month term unless either party provides sixty (60) days prior written notice.

12. INDEMNIFICATION & LIABILITY
Tenant agrees to indemnify Landlord against all claims and legal fees arising on the Premises.

13. GOVERNING LAW
Governed by the laws of the State of Illinois.

14. ENTIRE AGREEMENT
Constitutes the entire agreement between the parties.
